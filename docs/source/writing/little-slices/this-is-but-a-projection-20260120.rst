This is but a Projection \(2026-01-20\)
===================================================

Selenia yanked wires out of the contraption at an alarming rate, not helping Clemont's nervousness one bit.

"Selenia..." they hesitated. Task one, in their head, was getting their friend to calm down. "What are you looking for?"

"Looking for? I'm not looking for anything," came the reply, at so rapid a chatter that were it not Selenia, Clemont would have pegged it for being from someone in a, well, chemically altered state.

"So, you're ripping out cables... for fun?" they continued, scratching at their neck as a reflex reaction to the anxiety biting at them. They had seen Selenia like this once, when they'd both thought Selenia was losing their leg, but that was a long time ago—before either of them had received their implants.

"Just—just checking something, Clem." Selenia turned to face them, gray eyes meeting Clemont's red ones. They dropped whatever they'd just torn out of the device with a clang, chewing on their lip and seeming like they were searching Clemont somehow.

"What?" asked the target of the glare. Every moment that passed made things weirder. Selenia had gone from a frenzy to intensely still in the blink of an eye.

Selenia took a deep breath. "Clem," they started, their tone thoughtful, as if they were computing something. "I need you to do me a favor. Break off a piece of tile." They nodded toward those on the walls of Clemont's cell. "And throw it at me."

Clemont opened their mouth to ask if their friend had lost their mind, but was interrupted. "Just do it. Trust me."

The prisoner was not convinced, but the desperation in Selenia's eyes—something they'd rarely been witness to—pushed them to do it. Fortifying themselves, they elbowed a tile right in the middle, hissing at the pain. Still, they grasped for a shard, and, taking careful aim between the bars, threw it right at Selenia.

It flew right through their friend's shoulder.

Clemont blinked. "Did I miss?"

"No," responded Selenia, stressed renewed. "I'm not with you." They made eye contact with Clemont once more. "I'm just a projection."
