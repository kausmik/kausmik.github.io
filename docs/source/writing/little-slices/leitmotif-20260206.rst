Leitmotif \(2026-02-06\)
========================

.. admonition:: **Content Warnings**

   Mentions drugging.

Should you be the type to pay attention,
you'll find there are little things about the city that so distract you,
you forget the time of day.
I find she has a peculiar identity of her own.
In a way,
the city looks you in the eye,
curls a perfect eyebrow,
takes a drag of a cigarette held between practiced fingers,
and purrs,
"Can I help you?"

You just have to pay attention.

For better or for worse,
I get paid to do exactly that.
I'd therefore had this little song and dance with the city on more than one
occasion.
We even have our own tune, I like to think.
Then again, during many a dry, boring moment,
I'd wondered if I simply provide a modification to her theme;
that, say, were some other private detective in my place at this bar,
what she would sing with them would be ever so slightly different,
underlying melody unchanged.

A spot of movement catches my eye.
Tapping the ashes of my cigarette into an ashtray,
I train my eyes on a young-looking man across the way.
He's shorter than most,
probably about five-foot-three.
He seems the kind of man a proprietor would accuse of loitering, somehow.
There's something about him that screams, "Loiterer."
Maybe it's the way he leans against the wall, or
the fact he wears no coat; or
maybe it is the way his sleeves are rolled so casually beyond his elbow.
Perhaps it even has to do with the distinctive lack of a hat.

No.
Something's definitely off, but it isn't this man.
It isn't that he will be accused of loitering soon, either.
Without averting my gaze,
I put my cigarette out in the ashtray,
leaving it in and
leaning back in my booth,
trying to figure out what I'm missing.

A chill runs up my spine as someone whispers from much too close to my ear:
"Now, what's a nice private eye like you doing in a place like this?"
I feel a prick in my neck.

And all goes black.
