What You Can Expect in Little Slices¶

The following are the current guidelines I currently intend to follow as I write little slices. They encompass both rules for myself and a description of what little slices actually are, for the purpose of my writing.

If I do change them, I will keep older versions for posterity below, with the date of the last little slice published under those rules for reference.

Current Guidelines¶

  1. A little slice is a piece of English prose between 250 and 350 words in length (inclusive).

  2. While I make no promises that there will not be triggering content, I will endeavor to include content warnings in each piece (and if you find I have missed any, please feel free to contact me and tell me so!).

  3. Since little slices are so short, each will focus on a single meaningful moment, rather than on an entire story.

  4. Each little slice will be self-contained, i.e., all information required to read a piece will be part of said piece itself. There will be no mandatory external reading in order to understand any of them.
